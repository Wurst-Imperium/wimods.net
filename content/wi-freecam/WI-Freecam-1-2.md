## Changelog

- Added Polish translations (partial, ported from Wurst).

- Added Russian translations (partial, ported from Wurst).

- You can now take panorama screenshots while in Freecam. ([/d/1754](https://wurstforum.net/d/1754))

- Fixed Freecam in "Apply input to: Camera" mode leaking input packets, making you dismount vehicles and move minecarts when you're just trying to move the camera. (Minecraft 26.3 and later versions only)

- Enabling Freecam while spectating an entity now places the camera near the entity you were spectating, instead of teleporting it back to the player.
